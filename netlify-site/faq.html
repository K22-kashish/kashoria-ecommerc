Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about handmade crochet orders.

Are the products handmade?

Yes. KASHORIA products are presented as handmade crochet creations.

Can I request a custom colour?

Yes, where possible. Use the customization note on the product page or message us on WhatsApp before ordering.

When will my order ship?

Timing depends on the product and whether it is made-to-order. Confirm the expected dispatch date with KASHORIA before placing urgent orders.

Do you offer free delivery?

The storefront currently uses a ₹499 threshold for free delivery; otherwise the displayed delivery fee is ₹60.

Can I place bulk or gifting orders?

Yes. Message KASHORIA with your quantity, product preference and date so a custom quote can be discussed.

How do I pay online?

The current static checkout supports order capture and COD. A real online payment gateway such as Razorpay needs merchant credentials and server-side configuration.
