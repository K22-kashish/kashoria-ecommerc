Our story

Made by hand. Made to keep.

KASHORIA turns yarn, colour and tiny details into crochet pieces made for gifting, decorating and everyday joy.

Why handmade?

Every crochet piece takes time. That is the charm: small variations make each creation feel personal rather than factory-made.

We focus on cute, useful and giftable pieces — from charms and keychains to flowers, bouquets and accessories.

The KASHORIA promise

Thoughtful making, careful packing and friendly support. If you want a different colour, size or small custom touch, ask us before ordering.
